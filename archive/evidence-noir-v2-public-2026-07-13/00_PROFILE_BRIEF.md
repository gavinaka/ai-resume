# AkaGavin Personal Site Profile Brief

Last updated: 2026-07-11

This file is the profile anchor for the personal website in this folder. Any page copy, visual structure, and public positioning should be checked against this file before implementation.

## Core Positioning

AkaGavin is not presented as a traditional job seeker resume.

The public website should present:

- a personal decision system;
- a practical operating system for work, markets, assets, and AI workflows;
- a person who values verification, discipline, auditability, and system design;
- a B2B SaaS operator with long-term focus on trading systems, personal asset discipline, macro/business structure, and AI agent workflows.

Recommended public line:

> AkaGavin builds decision systems for business, markets, assets, and AI workflows.

Chinese version:

> AkaGavin 构建面向业务、市场、资产与 AI 工作流的个人决策系统。

## Narrative Thesis

The website should communicate one main idea:

> Real capability is not a list of skills. It is the ability to turn uncertainty into observable systems, repeatable processes, and reviewable decisions.

Chinese version:

> 真正的能力不是技能清单，而是把不确定问题拆成可观察、可执行、可复盘的系统。

- work as a stable cashflow base rather than the whole identity.

Avoid:

- sensitive employer details beyond broad background;
- internal company information;
- labor/legal history;

### 2. Trading System

Publicly show:

- exact profit target;
- current performance;

## Tone

The site should sound:

- direct;
- analytical;
- calm;
- mature;
- non-promotional;
- not like a generic job resume;
- not like a financial influencer profile.

Avoid:

- exaggerated self-branding;
- empty slogans;
- "10x", "growth hacker", "passionate builder" style language;
- public claims that cannot be verified.

## Visual Direction

Use:

- the original `Evidence Noir` direction: an interactive documentary and public evidence room;
- carbon black, cold paper, restrained archival blue and acid signal yellow;
- full-bleed portrait stills used as narrative scenes rather than desktop decoration;
- hard cuts, timecode, chapter rails, evidence rows and camera-like spatial transitions;
- an interactive relation room where movement reveals evidence relationships;
- compact but readable information density with visible source, method, boundary and verification;
- dedicated mobile sequencing rather than a scaled-down desktop metaphor.

Avoid:

- conventional resume template layout;
- desktop operating-system replicas, file icons, fake terminal launchers and pill navigation;
- decorative stock imagery;
- excessive gradients;
- cartoon illustrations;
- generic cyberpunk dashboards, glassmorphism and decorative motion without information value;
- overly luxurious finance styling;
- overly aggressive trading imagery.

## Public Profile Granularity

The site should not stop at broad labels such as "operator", "researcher", or "Agent builder". Public content must use six layers:

1. Identity coordinates: what role AkaGavin occupies in real life and in the system.
2. Experience path: how practical business, market discipline, and Agent engineering developed over time.
3. Operating traits: stable ways of thinking and acting.
4. Capability atoms: small, observable abilities rather than vague skill categories.
5. Project evidence: which public project demonstrates each capability.
6. Public boundary: what the evidence does not reveal.

Target inventory:

- 10 public identity coordinates;
- 5 experience stages;
- 12 operating traits;
- 24 capability atoms;
- 12 operating principles;
- 8 research areas;
- 10 or more project evidence dossiers.

Current Evidence Atlas inventory (2026-07-11):

- 42 public-safe evidence records;
- 20 experience events across 5 stages;
- 24 capability dossiers with definition, method, trigger, boundary, and evidence links;
- 12 operating-principle dossiers with origin, rule, trade-off, and failure signal;
- 11 project dossiers with project contract, evidence records, and capability mapping.

Evidence strength must remain explicit:

- `Artifact`: code, tests, architecture records, manifests, or delivered files;
- `Contract`: governance and operating contracts;
- `Profile`: stable user-confirmed statements, not independent audit proof;
- `Delivered`: behavior verified inside the public website project.

Do not use content volume as a substitute for evidence density. Every new public claim should either link to an evidence record or remain visibly marked as profile-backed or unverified.

Use progressive disclosure. The homepage carries identity signals, desktop windows carry profile detail, project pages carry evidence, and the infinite canvas carries relationships. Do not turn the mobile experience into a continuous wall of text.

## Project Evidence Contract

Every public project dossier must answer:

1. Why it exists.
2. What problem it solves.
3. AkaGavin's role in it.
4. Its key design principles.
5. What capabilities it proves.
6. What remains private or out of scope.

Do not infer a personal capability from a project unless the public role and design responsibility support that claim.
