# AkaGavin Personal Site Profile Brief

Last updated: 2026-07-05

This file is the profile anchor for the personal website in this folder. Any page copy, visual structure, and public positioning should be checked against this file before implementation.

## Core Positioning

AkaGavin is not presented as a traditional job seeker resume.

The public website should present:

- a personal decision system;
- a practical operating system for work, markets, assets, and AI workflows;
- a person who values verification, discipline, auditability, and system design;
- a B2B SaaS operator with long-term focus on trading systems, personal asset discipline, macro/business structure, and AI agent workflows.

Recommended public line:

> AkaGavin builds decision systems for business, markets, assets, and AI workflows.

Chinese version:

> AkaGavin 构建面向业务、市场、资产与 AI 工作流的个人决策系统。

## Narrative Thesis

The website should communicate one main idea:

> Real capability is not a list of skills. It is the ability to turn uncertainty into observable systems, repeatable processes, and reviewable decisions.

Chinese version:

> 真正的能力不是技能清单，而是把不确定问题拆成可观察、可执行、可复盘的系统。

## Public Pillars

### 1. Work Cashflow Base

Publicly show:

- B2B SaaS background;
- city or branch management experience;
- experience with small-business customers, sales systems, and operational pressure;
- work as a stable cashflow base rather than the whole identity.

Avoid:

- sensitive employer details beyond broad background;
- internal company information;
- labor/legal history;
- private compensation details.

### 2. Trading System

Publicly show:

- trading system as the main growth project;
- discipline, sample validation, process review, and risk control;
- liquidity and order-flow orientation;
- the principle that process quality is evaluated separately from outcome.

Avoid:

- exact capital size;
- exact profit target;
- current performance;
- live positions, order plans, or account details;
- any language that sounds like investment advice.

### 3. Asset Discipline

Publicly show:

- conservative personal finance discipline;
- long-term cashflow thinking;
- dividend and durable asset research as a framework;
- delayed expansion until the main system is validated.

Avoid:

- net worth;
- household income or expenses;
- specific holdings;
- order prices;
- private family asset structure.

### 4. AI Agent Workflow

Publicly show:

- multi-agent governance;
- read-only true sources;
- human-entered source data;
- deterministic calculation layer separated from AI judgment layer;
- auditability and low-drift workflows.

Avoid:

- token paths;
- API keys;
- Notion database IDs;
- private connector details;
- raw personal data.

### 5. Research Map

Publicly show:

- China macro;
- small-business survival and B2B SaaS;
- crypto market structure;
- A-share cashflow assets;
- AI workflows and personal automation.

Avoid:

- claiming expertise beyond evidence;
- making market predictions as certainty;
- presenting private research notes as public recommendations.

## Tone

The site should sound:

- direct;
- analytical;
- calm;
- mature;
- non-promotional;
- not like a generic job resume;
- not like a financial influencer profile.

Avoid:

- exaggerated self-branding;
- empty slogans;
- "10x", "growth hacker", "passionate builder" style language;
- public claims that cannot be verified.

## Visual Direction

Use:

- dark professional interface;
- research-terminal feel;
- decision-map and system-matrix layout;
- compact but readable information density;
- visible system relationships;
- responsive layout for mobile and PC.

Avoid:

- conventional resume template layout;
- decorative stock imagery;
- excessive gradients;
- cartoon illustrations;
- overly luxurious finance styling;
- overly aggressive trading imagery.

## Public Boundary

Default public name:

- AkaGavin

Do not publish unless explicitly confirmed:

- legal name;
- family details;
- exact asset numbers;
- live trading details;
- account balances;
- Notion IDs;
- API or token information;
- private files or paths;
- labor/legal history;
- employer-sensitive content.

## First-Version Website Structure

Recommended sections:

1. Hero: identity and core thesis.
2. Strategic Map: work, trading, assets, AI agents.
3. System Portfolio: what systems are being built.
4. Operating Principles: decision rules and governance.
5. Background: B2B SaaS and operator context.
6. Research Ledger: long-term research areas.
7. Contact: public links to be filled by user.

## Verification Checklist Before Publishing

- No exact financial numbers.
- No exact trading account data.
- No live positions or order details.
- No token, API, Notion, or connector details.
- No family private information.
- No employer-sensitive details.
- No investment advice tone.
- Mobile layout readable.
- PC layout readable.
- Content still reflects AkaGavin as a personal decision system, not a traditional resume.
